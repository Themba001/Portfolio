About Me

I’m Mabuza, a BSc Information Technology student at North-West University with a strong interest in backend engineering, cloud technologies, and building software that solves real-world problems.

Over the past year, I gained hands-on industry experience as a Software Development Engineer Intern at Amazon, where I worked on production systems, collaborated with experienced engineers, and contributed to software used at scale.

My experience includes backend development with Python, Java, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and cloud technologies, as well as building full-stack applications and deploying projects from development to production. I enjoy designing APIs, working with databases, improving system reliability, and writing clean, maintainable code.

In addition to my industry experience, I’ve mentored first-year students in Python and C++, helping them build a strong foundation in programming. I’m passionate about continuous learning and enjoy exploring software architecture, distributed systems, and scalable backend development.

I’m currently focused on growing into a software engineer who builds reliable, high-impact systems and contributes meaningfully to engineering teams.
