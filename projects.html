Emergency Shake App

Tap for more info

This mobile application was designed for students living off-campus. The user pre-profiles the app with their name, address, medical info, and emergency contacts. During a crisis, they don’t have to unlock the device — simply shake it.

The app detects the shake, gets the user's real-time location using GPS, and sends all the information to emergency responders instantly.

Built with: Flutter, Firebase, Google Maps API, Geolocation, Twilio API.
